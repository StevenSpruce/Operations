FE Teacher FTE Snapshot Collection

Collection requirements

Providers are asked to return their total teacher FTEs for staff on permanent or fixed-term contracts.

You must provide your total teacher FTE for the Monday of the third week in January.

Your count should:

  • include teachers employed on the snapshot date who are absent (for example, off sick or on leave)
  • exclude teachers who left employment before the snapshot date or started after the snapshot date

Definitions

FTE (Full-Time Equivalent)

This is the proportion of a standard week that a member of staff is contracted to work. 1.0 FTE is what the organisation deems to be ‘full time’. Where a member of staff has more than one contract, the FTE should be the combined percentage for all contracts.

Teacher

A teacher is a member of staff whose main role is a teaching role. Members of staff whose main role is not one of the teaching roles should not be included in the FTE count even if they also have a teaching role. Staff members should only be included if their main role is recorded in the FE workforce collection as:

  • Advanced Practitioner
  • Instructor
  • Lecturer
  • Practitioner
  • Teacher
  • Trainer
  • Tutor
